The primary setting is a high-magic fantasy planet often referred to as Pallimustus.

It is not a medieval backwater. It is:

Economically structured

Politically layered

Magically industrialized

Supported by adventurer infrastructure

Cities are protected by magic users. Trade routes are guarded. Essence items are regulated. This is a functioning magical civilization.

There are:

Kingdoms

Merchant city-states

Noble houses

Adventure guilds

Temples tied to gods

Magic is not rare. It is integrated into society.

⚙️ 2. The Magic System (The Engine of the World)

Everything revolves around:

Essences

Awakening Stones

Rank progression

Power tiers:

Iron

Bronze

Silver

Gold

Diamond

Each rank dramatically increases durability, magical potency, lifespan, and influence.

At higher ranks, individuals become walking natural disasters.

Gold and Diamond rankers shape geopolitics.

🧬 3. Essence Users vs Others

Not everyone is an adventurer.

There are:

Normal citizens (non-ranked)

Essence users (adventurers, nobles, elites)

Monsters (magical creatures, sometimes intelligent)

Essence users sit at the top of the food chain.

A single Silver ranker can dominate a battlefield.

A Diamond ranker can erase cities.

🏛 4. Political Structure

Power flows through:

Noble families who control essence vaults

Churches tied to gods

Adventure guilds regulating rankers

Merchant alliances

Awakening Stones are tightly controlled because creating new Iron rankers shifts power balances.

This makes essence acquisition political.

War often revolves around resource control.

🌌 5. The Gods

Yes, real gods.

Not distant metaphors.

Actual entities with:

Personalities

Agendas

Followers

Interventions

They operate through:

Blessings

Chosen champions

Divine items

Avatars

Some are benevolent.
Some are terrifying.
Some are alien beyond morality.

They cannot freely interfere without consequences.

There are cosmic rules even gods must respect.

🌑 6. Astral and Dimensional Structure

The world is not isolated.

There are:

Astral realms

Dimensional rifts

Other worlds (including Earth)

Cosmic entities beyond gods

The deeper the series goes, the more it becomes:

Political fantasy → interdimensional epic.

There are beings older than gods.

There are systems that regulate reality itself.

🧪 7. Monsters and Dungeons

Monsters are:

Natural magical fauna

Corrupted entities

Summoned beings

Astral intrusions

Dungeons are structured magical spaces:

Spawn monsters

Contain essence items

Serve as training grounds

Sometimes semi-sentient

Clearing dungeons fuels the economy.

⚖️ 8. Moral Landscape

This world is not bright heroic fantasy.

It deals with:

Slavery

Political corruption

Divine manipulation

Systemic exploitation

Power imbalances

Strength allows protection.

Strength also enables tyranny.

The tension is constant.

🧠 9. The Social Philosophy

The world rewards:

Preparation

Synergy

Strategic thinking

Team cohesion

Solo arrogance often gets you killed.

Teams dominate long-term survival.

This is why groups like Team Biscuit matter so much.

🌠 10. The Earth Connection

Earth exists.

Earth has no essence system.

When Earth becomes entangled with magic, chaos follows.

Earth politics + Pallimustus power scaling = instability.

This dual-world tension becomes a major axis of the narrative.

🔥 11. High-Rank Reality

At high tiers:

Space bends.

Concepts become weapons.

Souls are battlefields.

Death is sometimes negotiable.

Diamond rankers operate closer to myth than humanity.

Beyond Diamond lies something even less understood.

🧩 12. Core Theme of the World

At its heart, this world asks:

What happens when ordinary morality is placed inside extraordinary power?

That’s the spine of it.

Everything else is scaffolding.
